## Opening observation

Most organizations still talk about AI ambition more fluently than they talk about capability design.

## Interpretation

That gap matters. Strategy is not just choosing tools. It is deciding where judgment should sit, what data foundations need to exist, and how teams will actually use new capability in practice.

## Implication

For leaders, the useful question is rarely “how fast can we adopt AI?” It is “where will applied intelligence improve decisions, speed, or leverage without increasing confusion?”

## Closing line

The next advantage will come less from enthusiasm and more from disciplined capability building.
