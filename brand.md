# Fieldnotes Brand

## Core identity

- **Project name:** Fieldnotes
- **Subdomain:** `fieldnotes.databard.co.uk`
- **Primary domain reference:** `https://databard.co.uk/`
- **Tagline:** **Observations on strategy, intelligence, and coaching in an AI-shaped world**

## Purpose

Fieldnotes is a public-facing publication designed to build a distinctive voice through clear, thoughtful writing on:

- AI strategy
- data strategy
- coaching
- leadership
- strategic change

Its role is not to duplicate the main Databard site.
Its role is to demonstrate judgment, surface signal, and build trust through writing.

## Positioning

Fieldnotes should feel:

- premium
- intellectual
- practical
- commercially relevant
- reflective without sounding academic

It should sit adjacent to the main Databard brand while giving more space for an editorial, observational voice.

## Brand role

Fieldnotes exists to:

- build a recognizable point of view
- show strategic judgment in public
- strengthen trust in coaching and advisory services
- turn useful thinking into a body of work

## Homepage role

The homepage is a landing page and gallery.

It should:

- introduce what Fieldnotes is
- explain the kinds of writing published there
- surface recent or featured posts visually
- help visitors browse themes quickly

## Homepage structure

### Hero

**Fieldnotes**  
**Observations on strategy, intelligence, and coaching in an AI-shaped world**

Suggested intro:

> A public notebook of practical observations on AI, data, leadership, coaching, and strategic change. Built to surface signal, sharpen thinking, and make useful ideas easier to act on.

### Main content blocks

Use cards or gallery-style navigation for:

- **Daily Briefs** — short, timely observations on AI, data, coaching, and business change
- **Essays** — longer-form thinking on strategy, leadership, and modern advisory work
- **Themes** — grouped posts around recurring topics such as AI strategy, data capability, coaching, and transformation
- **Notes** — concise reflections, frameworks, and practical takeaways

### Post gallery

A visual grid of recent or featured posts showing:

- title
- summary
- date
- theme or tag

### Browse by theme

Initial themes:

- AI Strategy
- Data Strategy
- Coaching
- Leadership
- Strategic Change

## Brand principles

Fieldnotes should consistently express:

- signal over noise
- clarity over complexity theatre
- judgment over hot takes
- practical intelligence over hype
- thoughtful leadership over performative thought leadership

## Recommended navigation labels

- Home
- Briefs
- Essays
- Themes

## One-line brand summary

**Fieldnotes is a public notebook for thoughtful, practical observations on strategy, intelligence, and coaching in an AI-shaped world.**
